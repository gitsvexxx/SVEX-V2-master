Trading Fees

Transparent pricing for all your trades

At SVEX Trading, we believe in transparent and fair pricing. Our fee structure is designed to reward active traders while keeping costs competitive for everyone.

Standard Fee Structure

Transaction-Based Fees

Our fees are calculated based on your transaction size:

Transaction Amount Standard Fee Example
Less than €1,000 10% €500 trade = €50 fee
€1,000 or more 5% €2,000 trade = €100 fee

💡 How to Reduce Your Fees

We offer two ways to lower your trading fees:

  • High-Volume Trading: Complete more transactions to unlock lower fee tiers automatically
  • Contact Support: Reach out to our team to discuss custom fee arrangements for your trading needs

Volume-Based Discounts

The more you trade, the less you pay. Here's how our volume discount structure works:

Monthly Transaction Count Fee Discount New Fee Rate (€1,000+) New Fee Rate (<€1,000)
0-10 transactions 0% (Standard) 5% 10%
11-50 transactions 10% discount 4.5% 9%
51-100 transactions 20% discount 4% 8%
100+ transactions 30% discount 3.5% 7%

Need a Custom Fee Structure?

If you're a high-volume trader or have specific requirements, our support team can work with you to create a personalized fee arrangement.

Additional Information

No Hidden Fees

What you see is what you pay. There are no hidden charges, account maintenance fees, or surprise costs.

Fee Calculation

Fees are automatically calculated and deducted at the time of each transaction. You'll see the exact fee amount before confirming any trade.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our fee structure or want to discuss custom pricing, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
